#. Create a quotation or an invoice for a product subject to the FODEC.
#. The totals show the untaxed amount, then **FODEC 1%**, then the VAT computed on
   the untaxed amount plus the FODEC.
#. For an export sale, set the *Export* fiscal position on the customer or on the
   invoice: it removes the FODEC and maps the VAT to 0%.
#. The FODEC due is posted on account 436780 and reported on the *FODEC* line of
   the Tunisian tax report.
