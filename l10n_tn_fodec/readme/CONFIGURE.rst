#. Install the module on a database using the Tunisian chart of accounts
   (``l10n_tn``). The FODEC taxes are created for every Tunisian company, existing
   or new.
#. Only the products you **manufacture** and that are listed by decree are subject
   to the FODEC. On each of these products, add **FODEC 1%** next to the VAT in
   *Sales Taxes*.
#. On the bills of a supplier who invoices the FODEC, add **FODEC 1% (cost)** next
   to the VAT on the bill lines, or in the *Purchase Taxes* of the product.
