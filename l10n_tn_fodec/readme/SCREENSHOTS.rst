FODEC and VAT on the right base
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. image:: images/screenshot/invoice_fodec.png

Invoice of 1,000.000 TND: (1) FODEC 1% = 10.000, (2) VAT 19% computed on
1,010.000 = 191.900.

FODEC on the products you manufacture
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. image:: images/screenshot/product_taxes.png

Add FODEC 1% next to the VAT, only on the products subject to it.

Ready for the tax report
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. image:: images/screenshot/journal_items.png

(1) The FODEC is posted on account 436780 (other taxes on turnover) with the FODEC
tax grid, and is part of the VAT base. (2) The sale is tagged as turnover subject
to FODEC.
